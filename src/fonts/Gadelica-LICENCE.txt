Gadelica — a cló Gaelach (traditional Irish typeface)
© Séamas Ó Brógáin, 2007  <sob@iol.ie>

This directory bundles "Gadelica" (Gadelica.otf), used verbatim and unmodified
as the display face for Irish/Gaelic text in this project.

Distribution terms (from the font's own "Nóta", Feabhra 2007), translated:

  This font is distributed free of charge; it may be used without restriction
  and may be redistributed further. Copyright remains with its creator, and it
  is distributed on condition that you accept the following: that you will not
  alter the font in any way nor rename it; that you will not seek any payment
  for it; and that you will not present it as your own work or anyone else's
  other than its creator.

Accordingly the font here is the author's original .otf, byte-for-byte
unmodified (no subsetting, no format conversion, no renaming), and it is not
sold. Séamas Ó Brógáin is credited as its author.

Original Irish (Dáileadh):
  Tá an cló seo á dháileadh saor in aisce, ceadaítear é a úsáid gan bhac, agus
  ceadaítear é a dháileadh a thuilleadh. Fanann an cóipcheart, áfach, leis an té
  a chruthaigh é, agus dáiltear é ar choinníoll go nglacfaidh tú leis na
  coinníollacha seo a leanas: nach ndéanfaidh tú an cló a athrú ar bhealach ar
  bith ná a athainmniú; nach dtabharfaidh tú iarracht ar íocaíocht ar bith a
  fháil air; agus nach dtairgfidh tú é i leith is gur d'obair féin nó obair aon
  duine eile é seachas an té a chruthaigh é.
