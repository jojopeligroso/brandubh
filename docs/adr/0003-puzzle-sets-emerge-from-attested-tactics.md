# Puzzle sets emerge from attested tactics; most puzzles are simply tagged

The obvious way to organise a puzzle bank is to design a complete taxonomy and
sort every puzzle into it. We are not doing that, and a future reader will
notice that most of the bank sits in an unsorted pool.

The reason is that Brandubh's tactical vocabulary is not ours to invent. The
Hnefatafl forum's tactics threads already name Guillotine, Shuttle, Snap trap,
Clamp, Spring, Balling, Cordon, Corner fight and Twin towers, and Crust's note
that "the corner dynamics are the same as fetlar but with fewer pieces"
establishes which of them reach a 7×7 board. A taxonomy designed here would be
a parallel vocabulary competing with that one, and it would have to invent
names for the cases nothing was ever called.

So: recognisers are written only for attested terms, a **Named set** appears
only once enough puzzles carry its motif, and everything else is collected in
the **Pool** with computed tags. The set registry is data, so a new set is a
recogniser plus a name.

## Consequences

A recogniser that finds nothing is a result about Brandubh, not a bug — the
generator reports per-motif counts so "Twin towers: 0" is recorded rather than
silently absent. Expect the pool to hold the majority of the fifty, and expect
that to stay true: most real positions do not exhibit a named tactic.

Because a puzzle may have no motif, the completion note degrades in layers —
motif if there is one, otherwise the dominant evaluation term that moved,
otherwise a plain banded line. Each layer is a fixed template, so the note is
always something the system can actually justify.
