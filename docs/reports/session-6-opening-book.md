# Session 6 report — the Ollamh opening book

**Outcome: shipped.** Ollamh now answers the first two moves of each side
instantly, from a precomputed book, with measurably more opening variety and no
measured strength cost. Branch: `claude/brandubh-ollamh-opening-book-mu4jhs`
(merged with `main` as of `47a6d66`; all 490 tests green, build clean).

This report is the session-level narrative; the durable per-item record lives in
[`docs/ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md) (Session 6, marked shipped), with the honesty
notes in [`docs/solving.md`](../solving.md) §5.

## What was built

| Piece | Where |
|---|---|
| Offline book generator (deterministic, D4-folded, 4-way parallel) | `scripts/genbook.ts` |
| Bundled book data — 343 folded entries, 20 KB raw | `src/game/openingBook.data.ts` |
| Loader: unfolds all 8 D4 images at import → 2,737 runtime positions | `src/game/openingBook.ts` |
| Shared D4 symmetry group (one definition for engine, loader, generator) | `src/game/d4.ts` |
| Multi-PV root scorer (`scoreRootMoves`) + reworked `OPENING_BOOK`/`bookMove` | `src/game/ai.ts` |
| Measurement harness: latency, variety, gauntlets (paired + sharded) | `scripts/bookbench.ts` |
| Six book tests (legality fallthrough, rules gate, coverage, round-trip, seeded variety) | `src/game/ai.test.ts` |

Coverage is plies 0–3 — two interleaved "cones", because only positions the book
itself steers into are reachable through it: the booking side's plies keep its
best moves, the opponent's plies expand **every** legal reply. Deeper coverage
multiplies the frontier by ~(keep × replies) per two plies for moves the live
search already answers at mid-game speed, so it was deliberately not bought.

The shipped generation parameters are **margin 0** (exact ties only), **depth 8
uniform** (ollamh's measured live opening depth — never deeper), **cold
transposition table per position**. None of the three was in the original
sketch; all three were forced by measurement (below).

## Measured results

- **Speed:** booked reply in 0.1–0.8 ms vs 3.2–4.5 s live ollamh search (8 s
  budget, reaches depth 8). The slow, samey opening phase is gone.
- **Variety:** 20 distinct 4-ply openings / 18 distinct first moves over 24
  seeded games, vs 6 / 3 without the book — from exact root ties multiplied by
  the D4 unfold, not from booking a single deliberately-inferior move.
- **Strength:** depth-6 head-to-head (48 games, identical engine ± book):
  **book 28 — no-book 20**. Depths 3/5 saturate (all defender wins either way).
- **Bundle cost:** main 507.5 → 510.8 kB gzip, worker 5.8 → 8.4 kB — ~6 kB
  total against a ~150 kB budget.

## What the gauntlets actually taught (the real work)

Three candidate books were built and two were rejected on measurement:

1. **Margin 13** ("best ≤3 within ⅓ material unit for variety"): lost 7–17 at
   depth 4 — a move booked as "close to best" is still deliberately not-best,
   and the promise *"a book move is never weaker than what ollamh would have
   found"* is only true at margin 0. Rejected.
2. **Margin 0 with plies 0–1 searched one ply deeper**: paired depth-8 gauntlet
   got *worse* (6 same-seed games flipped against, 0 for). Investigating the
   surviving "ties" exposed **two real generator defects**, both fixed:
   - the multi-PV accept test compared against its integer-widened *window*
     rather than the margin, so fractionally-worse moves could pass as ties;
   - generation searched with a TT warmed by other positions — ordinary
     alpha–beta search instability under foreign bounds let a move the live
     engine rates **6 points worse** ship inside an "exact ties" entry.
3. **Shipped candidate** (margin 0, depth 8, cold): the paired depth-8 flip
   count *still* read 6-against — and turned out to be **byte-identical across
   all three candidates**, which no strength difference can produce. The
   flipped games diverge where the book's seeded pick lands on a different
   member of the *same verified-equal tie class* than the baseline's pick; under
   fresh seeds the roles invert (the book's "losing" member won both its games,
   the baseline's "winning" member lost one of two). Paired gauntlets stop
   measuring strength at the first value-equal divergence; the discriminating
   depth-6 head-to-head has the book ahead.

Two generalisations worth keeping: **a book must be generated by the exact
computation that will consume it** (same depth, cold table — "deeper is better"
fails when a shallower engine must play the follow-up), and **paired gauntlets
measure trajectory chaos, not strength, once the first divergence is a
value-tie**.

## Honesty relabeling

The pre-session `OPENING_BOOK` was documented as *solver-fed, proven moves
only*. This book is neither proven nor optimal, and is now labelled
*deep-search best-effort* everywhere (`ai.ts`, `docs/solving.md`, tests, the
in-game stats line shows "opening book" instead of a depth). What stays hard:

- entries are served only when the live ruleset **fingerprint-matches** the
  generation ruleset (WTF; walker/custom fall through to live search);
- every served move is **re-validated against the live legal moves** — a stale
  or corrupt book can only cause a silent fallthrough, never an illegal move;
- only `ollamh` consults the book; `easy`/`medium`/`hard` are untouched, and
  the Session-7 analysis search bypasses it by design.

## Rollback

The book is data behind a gate. Setting `BOOK_RULES_FINGERPRINT` to `""` in
`src/game/openingBook.data.ts` disables it entirely in one line (ollamh reverts
to live search); reverting the merge commit is the full undo.
